## Why Valor Mortis PlayStation Plus Searches Keep Coming Up

PlayStation Plus bundles several different things under one subscription. Some of them are permanent perks, like online multiplayer and cloud saves. Others are time-sensitive, like the monthly games you have to claim during a specific window, or catalog titles that can be removed when a licensing deal ends.

Because those time-sensitive pieces rotate, a game can be included one month, gone the next, and back again later. That rotation is the single biggest reason the Valor Mortis PlayStation Plus question doesn't have one permanent answer.

| Tier | What you get | Who it suits |
|---|---|---|
| Essential | Monthly games to claim, online multiplayer, cloud saves, member discounts | Players who mostly buy games outright but want online play |
| Extra | Everything in Essential plus the Game Catalog — a rotating library of PS4 and PS5 titles | Players who want a Netflix-style library |
| Premium | Everything in Extra plus Classics, game trials, and cloud streaming in supported regions | Players who want trials and older titles |

In practice, there are three separate routes to playing a game through the service: claiming it as a monthly title, downloading it from the Game Catalog, or using a time-limited trial. Any of those could apply to a given game — but only while it's actually listed.

For step five, the [official PlayStation Plus membership page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/ps-plus/) is the cleanest source, because it reflects your region rather than a generic global list.

Once you're on the store page, the label matters:

- **"Included with PlayStation Plus"** — accessible through the catalog or as a monthly title.
- **"Game Trial" or "Free Trial"** — a limited slice, usually tied to the Premium tier.
- **A price with a discount badge** — not included. It's simply on sale for members and non-members alike.

One more thing worth knowing: catalog lineups differ by region, and titles leave the service regularly. If a game is removed from the catalog, you lose access unless you purchased it separately. Monthly games work differently — claim them during their window and they stay in your library as long as your subscription is active.

## FAQ

**Is Valor Mortis on PlayStation Plus?**
It depends entirely on the current lineup in your region. **Which PS Plus tier do I need for it?**
Monthly games are available to all tiers, including Essential. Catalog titles require Extra or Premium, and game trials are typically Premium-only. If the game is only available as a trial in your region, you'll need the top tier.

**Can I play it on PS5 without a PlayStation Plus subscription?**
Yes. Buying the game digitally or on disc requires no subscription at all. A subscription only matters if you want to access it through the monthly games, the Game Catalog, or a trial.

**Does PlayStation Plus work the same on PS4?**
The tiers and benefits are the same, but catalog availability can differ between PS4 and PS5 versions of a title. Always check which console generation the listing covers before you subscribe or buy.
