## Combat Strategy: How to Beat the Valor Mortis Shrike Fight

### Watch the Telegraph, Not the Health Bar

New players stare at the enemy health bar. Experienced players stare at the shoulders, the weapon hand, and the feet. Those are where the real information lives. The health bar only tells you how long you have been fighting, which is exactly the number that makes people impatient.

### Control the Distance

Most aggressive characters want you at a specific range. If Shrike closes ground quickly, the correct answer is usually not to run — it is to step through the approach and end up behind or beside it. Backing away in a straight line is the single most common way players die, because it hands the initiative to the opponent for free.

### Punish, Do Not Pressure

There is a meaningful difference between attacking after a confirmed opening and attacking because you are nervous. One deals damage. The other deals damage to you. Land one or two hits per opening, then reset. Greed is a strategy only in highlight reels.

### Survive the Second Half

Fights like this one often change character as they progress. The pacing shifts, the pressure increases, and the moves you memorized in the first half stop being enough. Treat the second phase as a new fight with a new lesson, not as a victory lap.

| Common mistake | Why it fails | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| Mashing attack after a hit lands | You commit to an animation you cannot cancel | Count your hits out loud; stop at two |
| Panic healing at close range | Healing animations are slow and punishable | Create distance first, then heal |
| Dodging on reflex instead of on cue | Wasted stamina leads to a stun and a death | Dodge when the attack commits, not when it starts |
| Changing builds mid-fight | You lose familiarity exactly when you need it | Commit to one setup for at least five attempts |
| Ignoring audio cues | Many attacks announce themselves before they animate | Play with headphones and listen for the wind-up |
